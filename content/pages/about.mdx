## Hello, I am Tanya

I am Greek-Palestinian, I grew up in Greece, and today I live and work in Dubai. I have always been drawn to the places where two cultures meet: long tables, shared bread, the sea close by, and time to sit with the people you love.

Ef Zin grew from that. I trained as a civil engineer, and I still love a well-made structure, but I found that the things that hold a life up are quieter: how we eat, how we move, how we rest and who we spend our days with. Ef Zin is my way of building gatherings around them.

## What Ef Zin means

Ef Zin comes from the ancient Greek εὖ ζῆν, "living well". For the Greeks it was not a luxury or a project. It was a way of paying attention, and it still is. Our tagline holds the whole idea: Ancient Wisdom for Modern Living.

## The manifesto

We hold five values close, and every experience we create is shaped by them.

- **εὐδαιμονία (eudaimonia), flourishing.** A life that feels full, not merely busy.
- **αρμονία (harmonia), harmony.** Body, mind and the people around us in tune.
- **νοῦς (nous), mental clarity.** Room to think, breathe and see what matters.
- **σῶμα (soma), physical health.** Strength and ease, built gently and kept.
- **ψυχή (psyche), soul.** Joy, stillness and meaning, in whatever shape they come.
