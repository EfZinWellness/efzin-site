These terms describe how booking with Ef Zin Wellness works. By booking, you confirm that you have read and accepted them, together with the cancellation policy shown for your experience at checkout. A snapshot of that policy is saved with your booking, so later changes never affect a booking you have already made.

## Events (Dubai day experiences)

- More than 48 hours before the start: a full refund (minus Stripe processing fees) or full-value credit, your choice.
- Within 48 hours of the start: credit only.
- No-show: no refund and no credit.
- You may transfer your place to a friend for free, up to 2 hours before the start.

## Retreats

- More than 60 days before arrival: a full refund of the amounts you have paid, minus Stripe processing fees.
- 30 to 60 days before arrival: 50% of the total retreat price is refunded. This is calculated as the amount you have paid minus 50% of the total price (never less than zero), minus Stripe processing fees.
- Within 30 days of arrival: no refund. You may transfer your place to another person.
- The balance is due 10 days before arrival, whether you pay in one payment or monthly. Anything unpaid at that point cancels the booking under these rules.
- Payment plans: cancelling stops all future payments. Refunds are calculated on the amounts you have actually paid, using the rules above. Ef Zin does not charge outstanding payments after a cancellation.

## Stripe processing fees

Stripe charges a processing fee on every card payment, and Ef Zin cannot recover it. When you cancel and are entitled to a refund, that fee is deducted from the refund. You always see the exact amount before you confirm.

## If Ef Zin cancels

If we cancel an experience, you receive a full refund with no fee deducted, or you may transfer your booking to a future experience, whichever you prefer.

## Credit

Credit is held in AED and can be used on any event or retreat. It expires 12 months after it is issued, and we send a reminder 30 days before it does.

## Gift cards

- A gift card is valid for 12 months from the date of purchase, and we send a reminder 30 days before expiry. Any value left at expiry is kept by Ef Zin.
- It can be used on any event or retreat, in full or in part. The remaining balance stays on the card.
- Gift cards are not exchangeable for cash, and cannot be used to buy other gift cards.
- A gift card is refundable within 14 days of purchase if it has not been used. After that, or once any part has been used, it is not refundable.
- If you pay for a booking partly with a gift card and the policy allows money back, the gift card portion is returned to the gift card first.

## Private and corporate experiences

Bespoke private and corporate experiences are agreed individually, and the cancellation terms are set in your written proposal.

## Health and responsibility

Our sessions are gentle, but you take part at your own pace and at your own risk. Please tell us about any condition, injury or pregnancy before you join, and listen to your body throughout.
