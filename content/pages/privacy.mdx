Ef Zin Wellness cares about your privacy. This page explains what we collect, why we collect it, who helps us look after it, and the choices you have.

## What we collect

- **Contact and booking details:** your name, email, phone number and the experiences you book, so that we can run your booking and look after you.
- **Health information:** when you choose to share it, such as conditions, injuries, pregnancy, dietary needs and a signed waiver, so that we can keep you safe. Health data is special category data, so we ask for your explicit consent and keep it in a restricted area that only you and the Ef Zin team can see. In the UAE and, for guests in the EU and UK, under the GDPR, it receives the highest level of protection.
- **Enquiries:** what you tell us in the Bespoke, coaching and retreat forms.
- **Marketing preferences:** newsletter consent is recorded separately from booking emails. We only send marketing if you say yes, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

We never put personal data in web addresses.

## Who processes your data

- **Stripe** handles all payments. Card details go directly to Stripe and never reach our servers.
- **Resend** delivers our emails, such as confirmations and reminders.
- **Supabase** hosts our database and accounts, with row-level security so that members can only see their own records.

We do not sell your data.

## Your choices

You can see and update your details in My Ef Zin, withdraw consent at any time, and request a copy or the deletion of your data from your account or by writing to us. We will keep only what the law requires us to keep, such as payment records.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while you hold an account and for as long as needed for your bookings and our legal obligations. Deletion requests are handled promptly.

## Contact

For anything about your data, write to contact@efzinwellness.com.
