Ef Zin experiences include yoga and movement, breathwork, contrast therapy (sauna or other heat, and ice baths or other cold), and shared food. Please read this before you join. By ticking the box, you confirm that you have read it, understood it and agree to it.

## Your health

- You will tell us about any medical condition, injury, pregnancy, allergy or dietary need that could affect you, and keep your health details up to date in My Ef Zin.
- If you have a heart or blood pressure condition, epilepsy, asthma or another breathing condition, diabetes, a recent injury or surgery, or you are pregnant, you will check with your doctor before taking part in breathwork, heat or cold exposure, or strenuous movement.
- You will not take part under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

## Taking part

- Every activity is optional. You can rest, adapt a pose, shorten your time in heat or cold, or step out at any point, and you will do so if you feel faint, dizzy, unwell or in pain.
- You will follow the instructions of the Ef Zin team and our partner venues, especially the time limits for sauna and ice baths.
- Breathwork can cause tingling, light-headedness or strong emotions. Do not practise it in water, while driving, or standing where a fall could hurt you.
- Heat and cold put real stress on the body. Drink water, and leave the sauna or ice bath straight away if you feel unwell.

## Food

- We plan menus around the allergies and dietary needs you share with us, but our partner kitchens may handle nuts, gluten, dairy, shellfish and other allergens. We cannot guarantee that any dish is free of trace allergens.
- If you have a severe allergy, bring your own medication (for example an adrenaline pen) and tell us on arrival.

## Risk and responsibility

- You understand that physical activity, breathwork, and heat and cold exposure carry some risk of injury or illness, and you take part voluntarily and at your own risk.
- To the extent the law allows, Ef Zin Wellness, its team and its partner venues are not liable for injury, illness, loss or damage arising from your participation, except where caused by our negligence or where the law does not allow us to limit liability.
- You are responsible for your own belongings.

## Emergencies

If you need medical help during an experience, you agree that the Ef Zin team may call emergency services and share the health details you have given us with them.

## Photos

We sometimes take photos at experiences. If you would rather not appear, tell us on the day and we will respect that.
