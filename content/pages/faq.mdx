## Booking

**How do I book?** Choose an event or retreat and press Book. You can also enquire first if you would like to talk it through. Your account is created automatically when you book, so there is nothing to set up beforehand. You will find your bookings, details and credit in My Ef Zin.

## Payment

**How do I pay?** Prices are shown in your local currency at checkout, and you pay securely by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. For retreats, a deposit and monthly payment plans are available, so you can spread the cost.

## Cancellations

**What if my plans change?** The policy depends on the experience and is shown on every event page and at checkout. In short: for events, more than 48 hours before the start you can have a refund (minus Stripe processing fees) or full-value credit; within 48 hours, credit only. For retreats, the earlier you tell us the more you receive back. If Ef Zin cancels, you always get a full refund or a transfer. The full details are in our [Terms](/terms).

## Retreat pricing

**What is included in a retreat?** Accommodation, meals and all the sessions are included. Flights are not. We are happy to help arrange local transfers if you would like us to.

## Private and corporate

**Can you create something just for us?** Yes. Bespoke experiences are designed for individuals, couples, families, groups of friends and companies, in person, virtual or hybrid. Tell us about it on the [Bespoke page](/bespoke) and Tanya will reply personally.

## Gift cards

**How do gift cards work?** A gift card is valid for 12 months and can be used on any event or retreat. The recipient does not need an account to spend it.
