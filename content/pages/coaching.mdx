## A reset that fits your life

Coaching with Tanya is a personal, unhurried programme to help you reset the everyday: what you eat, how you move and the small habits that shape your energy. There is no template and no strict plan. We begin by listening, then build something you can actually keep.

## What we work on

- **Nutrition.** Simple, Mediterranean-inspired eating that nourishes you and fits your schedule and tastes.
- **Movement.** Strength, mobility and calm, drawing on yoga and Lagree, at a pace that suits your body.
- **Habits.** Sleep, stress, rhythm and routine, adjusted gently, one step at a time.

## How it works

Programmes are tailored to you and can be in person in Dubai, fully online, or a mix of both. Tell us a little about yourself below, or message Tanya on WhatsApp, and we will suggest a first conversation.
