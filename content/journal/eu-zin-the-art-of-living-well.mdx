Our name is two Greek words, εὖ ζῆν, which together mean "to live well". It is a gentle phrase, and an old one. Aristotle used it to describe a life of flourishing, not a life of comfort.

## More than pleasure

For the Greeks, living well was never only about pleasure. It was about εὐδαιμονία, a sense of flourishing that comes from living in tune with who you are: caring for the body, keeping the mind clear, and sharing a table with good company.

## Small, daily, repeatable

The beautiful thing is how ordinary it is. A slow breakfast. A walk after dinner. Olive oil on everything. A friend who stays for another coffee. None of it needs a big plan, only attention.

## Try this week

- Eat one meal without a screen.
- Move your body in a way that feels kind, for ten minutes.
- Say yes to one long conversation.

That is εὖ ζῆν. The rest is practice.
