This is the salad we put in the middle of the table: sharp lemon, wild oregano, sweet tomatoes and good olive oil. It tastes better after an hour, which makes it ideal for a lunch you want to enjoy rather than rush.

## You will need

- 2 cups cooked chickpeas, drained
- 3 ripe tomatoes, chopped
- 1 cucumber, diced
- half a red onion, finely sliced
- a generous handful of parsley, chopped
- 1 teaspoon dried Greek oregano
- juice of 1 lemon
- 4 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- sea salt and black pepper
- feta, to serve (optional)

## Method

1. Soak the onion in cold water for ten minutes, then drain. It softens the bite.
2. Whisk the lemon juice, olive oil, oregano, salt and pepper in a large bowl.
3. Add the chickpeas, tomatoes, cucumber, onion and parsley, and toss gently.
4. Rest for at least 30 minutes. Crumble over feta if you like, and serve with warm bread.

Serves four, as a starter or alongside fish.
